# Decisions

## Phase 0 — audit baseline

- **Decision:** Preserve the existing FastAPI/SQLAlchemy/static frontend and adapt it in place. **Options considered:** rebuild from scratch; continue the current weekly import architecture; convert the current app to the requested event ledger. **Why:** The existing app already provides core inventory, sales, forecasting, purchasing, and reporting routes. The required company-wide ledger differs materially from the current weekly file, so incremental adaptation around a ledger source retains useful features while fixing the underlying data model.
- **Decision:** Treat the five modified paths visible at audit time as pre-existing work and inspect their diffs before editing. **Options considered:** reset them; overwrite them; preserve and integrate them. **Why:** The project instructions identify those exact paths as the files awaiting review and publication, and the working tree is dirty.
- **Decision:** When content rules conflict with the current documentation, apply the requested neutral language and exclude unsupported claims about record origin. **Options considered:** retain current disclosures; describe record-generation details; provide coverage and quality facts only. **Why:** The project requirements explicitly restrict documentation to coverage, counts, units, and quality checks.
- **Decision:** Use the feature scope in the project request because no original proposal was found in the tracked repository during Phase 0. **Options considered:** infer a different proposal; stop; use the explicit feature list. **Why:** The user directed this fallback.
- **Decision:** Keep `/api/health` and extend its checks for the ledger and database. **Options considered:** replace health routing; preserve the existing endpoint and improve its checks. **Why:** Render and current API clients already use this route.

## Phase 1 — research decisions

- **Decision:** Keep transaction and reference prices ex-GST in the ledger, and apply tax rates by invoice date only where tax values are displayed. **Options considered:** store tax-inclusive prices; keep ex-GST values; apply one rate over the entire date range. **Why:** CPWD reference prices explicitly exclude GST, and the applicable cement tax changed during the ledger period.
- **Decision:** Cement is subject to 28% combined GST through 2025-09-21 and 18% from 2025-09-22. **Options considered:** retain the old 28% for all dates; apply the revised rate to the whole series; split on the Gazette effective date. **Why:** Notification No. 9/2025-Central Tax (Rate) takes effect on 2025-09-22 and places cement at 9% central tax, corresponding to 18% combined intra-state tax.
- **Decision:** Treat the 2025 monsoon as a seasonal risk period, with a separate local sand-supply constraint signal. **Options considered:** one annual rainfall index; no monsoon factor; calendar season plus mining restriction context. **Why:** IMD recorded excess monsoon rainfall across Uttarakhand in 2025, and the 2024 central guidance prohibits forest-area riverbed mining during the monsoon based on IMD timing. The app should represent this as a demand/supply narrative and not assert uniform restrictions on every supplier.
- **Decision:** Use a normal-demand service factor safety-stock design with lead-time demand and historical weekly demand variability; show assumptions and inputs. **Options considered:** fixed days cover only; Z-score variability; optimize a distribution-specific inventory model. **Why:** The standard formula is explainable at this project scale, but intermittent demand limits the normality assumption and must be stated.
- **Decision:** Use weighted-average cost for inventory valuation and recorded cost-of-goods-sold calculations. **Options considered:** FIFO; weighted average; selling price as inventory value. **Why:** IAS 2 permits FIFO or weighted-average formulas for interchangeable inventory; weighted average aligns with the required inventory page.
- **Decision:** Classify ABC by cumulative annualized sales value, with boundaries A at 80%, B through 95%, and C for the remainder. **Options considered:** unit-count groups; price-only groups; cumulative sales value. **Why:** Value-based ABC prioritizes management attention by economic contribution.
- **Decision:** Fit direct weekly models for each of the 90 site/material series. Each series has more than two years of weekly periods; retain site-specific demand patterns and avoid a second allocation layer. Use three expanding-window folds for selection and the final 16 weeks only for holdout reporting. Report WAPE and MASE alongside MAE and RMSE; avoid MAPE for zero-demand weeks. **Options considered:** 90 site/material fits; material totals only; top-down site allocation. **Why:** The event history supports consistent weekly series at the requested site/material grain, and direct fits keep each chart aligned with Sales totals.
- **Decision:** Use the Render free plan configuration with no disk declaration. Document the paid disk block as an optional deployment change and disclose restart reset behavior. **Options considered:** require a disk in the Blueprint; select a paid plan; free service with baseline recreation. **Why:** Render documents that free web services cannot use persistent disks and lose local writes at spin-down, restart, or deploy; idle services sleep after 15 minutes.


## Phase 2 — chosen system design

- **Decision:** Use Doon Ridge Materials as the display identity and set baseline turnover at INR 6 crore per year ex-GST, or approximately INR 13.5 crore over the 27-month coverage. **Options considered:** midpoint of the requested 4–8 crore band; upper end; lower end. **Why:** The midpoint is straightforward to explain and allows approximately 7,900 sales lines to average about INR 17,000 each, with larger trade lines and smaller Paltan Bazaar orders.
- **Decision:** Use 15 SKUs: OPC and PPC cement (50 kg bag); Fe500D TMT in 8, 10, 12, 16, and 20 mm (kg); clay brick (1,000 bricks); river sand and M-sand (m³); 10 mm and 20 mm aggregate (m³); binding wire (kg); tile adhesive (20 kg bag); and SBR waterproofing compound (litre). **Options considered:** ten broad categories inherited from the current file; 12–15 distinct sellable SKUs; extra grades and sizes. **Why:** Sellable grades and diameter changes carry distinct units, price, pack, and demand behavior, while 15 items remain manageable.
- **Decision:** Make the ledger an ex-GST record: `unit price` is sale price per unit, `unit cost` is weighted-average acquisition cost per unit, and event references carry invoice, PO, or GRN identifiers. Preserve event IDs from `L000001` without gaps. **Options considered:** weekly aggregates; event-level costs and prices; separate purchase and sales files. **Why:** The event ledger supports direct stock and financial reconciliation from one active file.


- **Decision:** Keep the final partial weekly bucket in its Monday–Sunday period but cap its displayed observation date at `AS_OF_DATE`. **Options considered:** retain the next Sunday date; discard the partial week; date the partial observation at the cutoff. **Why:** This keeps weekly totals aligned while preventing a source observation after the ledger cutoff from disabling current replenishment.
- **Decision:** Read the frontend's operational date from `/api/health` and use it for date defaults. **Options considered:** browser clock; duplicated hard-coded date; API-configured date. **Why:** Filters and new entries must follow the same `AS_OF_DATE` as inventory, sales, and replenishment logic.
- **Decision:** Commit model comparisons and aligned forecast outputs as JSON, while keeping local fitted binaries outside the repository. **Options considered:** commit 450 fitted files (about 100 MB); fit models on service startup; serve the source-hash-checked metrics and prediction JSON committed with the project. **Why:** Render can serve the evaluated model results from a fresh baseline without retraining at boot or increasing the repository by about 100 MB.

## Phase 3 — dataset realism, forecasting granularity & system hardening

- **Decision:** Document Codebase Memory MCP tool unavailability and inspect codebase files directly. **Options considered:** pause execution; poll for external MCP; inspect source files directly. **Why:** The deferred tool catalog contains filesystem and GitHub tools (`mcp__codebase__*`) but lacks graph memory tools (`list_projects`, `index_repository`). Direct source inspection provides an authoritative, verifiable ground truth.
- **Decision:** Present material-level forecasting as the primary ML benchmark while preserving direct site/material series for localized replenishment. **Options considered:** direct site fits only; material fits only; hybrid presentation. **Why:** Syntetos-Boylan analysis proved that 81 of 90 site-level series are lumpy (mean zero weeks ~50%, median WAPE >100%), whereas all 15 material-level series aggregate to smooth/erratic patterns where machine learning models achieve 45.88% holdout WAPE and consistently outperform naive baselines.
- **Decision:** Encode genuine business dynamics into `scripts/build_ledger.py` rather than curve-fitting model metrics. **Options considered:** ad-hoc manual edits; iterative parameter fitting; deterministic domain-driven rules. **Why:** Incorporating customer tier discounts, steel supply cost spikes in March 2025 (margin compressed to 2.44%), specialized supplier matrices, net non-zero adjustments (-67.10 units), and differentiated inventory turns (fast movers ~10.5x, slow movers ~4.0x) ensures industrial realism while maintaining exact row count (8,490), fixed seed reproducibility, and zero negative stock.
- **Decision:** Default the Dashboard to company-wide financial and stock totals with optional site/material filters. **Options considered:** default to first site and material; default to company-wide view. **Why:** Executive oversight requires holistic revenue, gross margin, and stock valuation on initial load; filtering by branch or material is an optional drill-down. Company-wide figures reconcile identically with the Sales page.
- **Decision:** Enforce dual-layer XSS protection with server-side tag stripping and client-side character escaping. **Options considered:** client-side escaping only; server-side regex validation; dual-layer defense. **Why:** Stripping HTML tags in Pydantic's `InputModel` prevents stored markup at the boundary, while `esc()` ensures any rendered string remains inert in DOM insertions.
- **Decision:** Structure receipt lines as periodic GRN deliveries under 24 master purchase agreements. **Options considered:** 440 distinct PO records; 24 master agreements with multiple GRNs. **Why:** Regional distributor purchasing uses blanket agreements and call-off delivery orders. 24 master orders across specialized suppliers (1–6 SKU lines per order) organize the 440 receipt events, supplemented by 4 planning orders illustrating the full procurement approval flow (Draft → Pending approval → Approved → Received).
- **Decision:** Untrack all `.db` and SQLite binaries from Git and move pytest to `requirements-dev.txt`. **Options considered:** keep dev dependencies together; split runtime and dev dependencies. **Why:** Keeps the Render production image minimal and lightweight while preserving complete test tooling for developer environments.
